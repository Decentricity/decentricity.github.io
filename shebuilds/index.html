Not a pitch deck hackathon.

She Builds is about crossing the gap between “I have an idea” and “here is the link.” AI has changed who can make software. This hackathon is built around that fact.

You do not need to arrive as a professional developer. You need curiosity, an idea worth testing, and the nerve to actually ship it. Use AI-assisted development to turn your concept into a working, shareable product.

What you get

A practical runway from first prompt to functioning product, with tools, visibility, and a reason to finish.

01 · Build

Berrry.app credits

Participants receive 30 days of Berrry credits to prototype, build, iterate, and publish a real app.

02 · Show

A finalist stage

Selected finalists get the opportunity to demo their products and defend the thinking behind them at a FrenchTech event.

03 · Grow

More runway for winners

Three winning projects receive an extended 90-day Berrry Pro package to keep building beyond the hackathon.

FAQ

Yes, you can actually do this.

Do I need to be a software developer?

No. The hackathon is specifically designed around AI-assisted development, which makes software creation accessible to people with many different backgrounds. Technical experience is useful, but it is not the admission ticket.

What do I need to submit?

A working, shareable app that can be opened through a URL, plus the information requested during the hackathon submission process.

Can I participate with a team?

Yes. You can build individually or with a team.

What should I build?

Something useful, interesting, delightful, strange, ambitious, practical, or all of the above. Strong entries turn a clear idea into a product people can genuinely interact with.

Who is behind She Builds?

She Builds is organized in collaboration between FrenchTech Indonesia and Berrry.app.
