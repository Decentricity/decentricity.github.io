Ideas should become things.

She Builds is about reducing the distance between “I have an idea” and “I made this.” AI-assisted development means you do not need a conventional software background to start building useful, ambitious products.

Bring curiosity, a problem worth solving, and the willingness to ship. The goal is a working product, not a perfect pitch deck.

What you get.

A practical runway to build independently, experiment with AI tools, and put a real product in front of people.

01 · Build

AI-assisted creation

Use Berrry.app and other AI-assisted workflows to move from concept to working software faster.

02 · Ship

Make it real

Work independently from home throughout October and submit a functional, shareable product rather than a concept alone.

03 · Show

Finalist showcase

Selected finalists get the chance to present their work on 10 November and put their product in front of the La French Tech community.

How it works.

No weekend lock-in, no need to camp under fluorescent lights eating instant noodles. The main build happens independently, at home, across October.

01

Register in September

Complete the registration form anytime during September 2026. You can enter individually or as a team.

02

Build throughout October

Develop your project independently from home during October. Participants receive Berrry credits to help turn ideas into working applications.

03

Submit your product

Ship something functional and shareable so the judging team can evaluate what you actually built.

04

Finals · 10 November

Selected finalists present their projects, explain the thinking behind them, and compete for the final awards.
