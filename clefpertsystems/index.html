01 / The opportunity

The overlooked training corpus
is hiding in the rules.

For decades, experts translated judgment into software: eligibility rules, diagnostic pathways, industrial assessments, operational policies. That knowledge still runs. It can also teach.

An executable knowledge base is a compact generator of labeled experience. Enumerate valid situations, ask the system what follows, and keep the decision alongside its proof path.

ClefpertSystems explores whether a neural student can learn that behavior, then earn its place by being tested against the symbolic teacher. The long-term opportunity is a migration layer for institutional intelligence: domain knowledge carried forward into adaptable decision software.

Read the reconstruction methodology ↗
A

Knowledge as supervision

A rule base defines how decisions change across a domain. Run it across valid cases to reveal that behavior as training examples.

B

A teacher that stays available

Keep the oracle after training. Check agreement, inspect disagreements, and rerun regression cases when the source knowledge changes.

C

A route out of legacy silos

Reusable adapters could carry Prolog, Datalog and other rule systems into a common learning pipeline, while retaining their origins.
