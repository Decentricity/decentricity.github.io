## Core argument

The singularity may not look like one increasingly general model crossing a threshold. It may look more like the Cambrian explosion: rapid diversification into many forms of cognition built from models, expert systems, fast deciders, memory, tools, biological architectures, agents, humans, and eventually hybrid substrates.

The closing line is:

> Maybe the singularity isn't one mind waking up. Maybe it's a biosphere.
