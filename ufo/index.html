What Stood Out

The strongest material is not one isolated image. It is the combination of mission reports, DVIDS source pages, sensor video, FBI interviews, NASA transcripts, and older intelligence files.

Modern sensor cases

PR34 and PR28 are the first reads: Greece sensor-video cases with mission-report context, unusual motion descriptions, and public DVIDS media.

Witness-heavy modern records

The Western U.S. slides and USPER statement are high-strangeness modern accounts, but they are also heavily redacted and should be read as unresolved evidence, not proof.

Historical depth

Apollo transcripts, Socorro, WWII foo-fighter files, and the 1955 Russell report show continuity across agencies and decades.
