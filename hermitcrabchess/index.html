    Hermit Crab Chess Rules

    • Unshelled kings are normal kings: they can be checked, cannot move into check, and can be checkmated.
    • A king can spend its turn attaching one adjacent allied non-king piece. The piece leaves the board and becomes the king's shell.
    • A shelled king moves only like its shell piece. It does not use normal king moves and ignores check/checkmate.
    • Shelled kings must be captured directly. Capturing a shelled king ends the game immediately.
    • This prototype supports shell replacement. Detaching is omitted: once attached, a shell stays until replacement or death.
    • Castling and en passant are disabled. Pawns auto-promote to queens. There is no AI opponent or online multiplayer.
