    Hermit Crab Chess Rules

    • Unshelled kings can be checked and cannot move into check. In this variant, a checked unshelled king is checkmated when the king itself has no legal king move.
    • A king can spend its turn attaching one adjacent allied non-king piece, but only if the king is not currently in check. The king moves onto that piece's square, and the piece becomes the king's shell.
    • A shelled king moves only like its shell piece. It does not use normal king moves and ignores check/checkmate.
    • Shelled kings must be captured directly. Capturing a shelled king ends the game immediately.
    • Once a king has a shell, it cannot attach another piece. Click the shelled king twice to unshell: the shell piece lands on a legal square for that piece, and the king stays behind unshelled.
    • Castling and en passant are disabled. Pawns auto-promote to queens. There is no AI opponent or online multiplayer.
