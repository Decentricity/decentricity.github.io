Music description

Global Metadata
Euphoric electronic rock and synth-pop anthem, 140 BPM, 4/4, approximately four minutes. Urgent, irreverent, emotionally sincere accelerationism. The narrative moves from present-day machine learning to digital immortality and an inhabited galaxy. Driving electronic drums, sequenced bass, bright arpeggios, distorted guitars, expansive synthesizers. Increasing rhythmic density and harmonic scale create continuous forward momentum.

Vocal Details
Confident female alto, crisp consonants, rhythmically precise verses, powerful melodic choruses. Playful delivery of technical references; sincere, intimate delivery of the immortality bridge. Sing “R L H F” as four separate letter names and “E slash acc” as “ee slash ack.” Pronounce Kardashev “kar-duh-SHEV.” Give “digital immortality” a full melodic phrase. Stacked vocal harmonies grow across choruses; group vocals reinforce “We intend to stay.”

Arrangement
Short instrumental intro with sequencer and immediate drum pulse. First two verses use tight bass, dry vocals, and accumulating percussion. Pre-chorus adds rising arpeggios; chorus opens into full drums, guitars, and wide harmonies. Third verse retains momentum and adds an octave bass layer. Bridge brings the lead vocal forward over a persistent driving pulse. Fourth verse expands into cosmic synth textures. The following pre-chorus becomes a chant with snare rolls and rising tension. Final chorus delivers the largest arrangement. Outro keeps full rhythmic momentum beneath the final exchange, ending on a decisive sustained chord.
