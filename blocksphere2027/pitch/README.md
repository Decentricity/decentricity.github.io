# Beyond the slide

Published at https://decentricity.github.io/blocksphere2027/pitch/.

A deliberately ordinary company slide opens into a ten-chapter, full-screen WebGL presentation. The sequence retains the original's slide-to-tower portal, ascent through the sky, ocean fold, swarm, domino impact, lamp, product world, retrospective wall, and wall-to-closing-slide portal. A newly authored portfolio detonation follows the swarm.

Click or press Right to advance; Left to go back; A toggles autoplay; M toggles sound. The small Story & portfolio control opens the reading edition and sources. Reduced-motion users start with the reading option.

The animation engine and nine scene renderers are adapted from the original presentation archived in `../source/`. Attribution, license and provenance are preserved there. `spectacle.js` contains the new scene. Rebuild the adapted engine with `node source/build.cjs`; the source archives and parser are shared with the parent directory. All assets are local or inherited from the parent presentation.

Content reflects the portfolio archive and public profiles checked 26 November 2026. Landscapes, explosions and chart heights are illustrative. Portfolio count: 37 archived engagements since 2017. See the reading edition for qualifications and source links.
