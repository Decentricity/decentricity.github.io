The old internet, rescued.

A map of the original Decentri.city.

The original site sprawled across decentralization reviews, transhumanism, blockchain-era talks, virtual worlds, robots, and a personal press archive. The modern homepage became a much smaller bio; this archive puts the old material back in one readable place without pretending it was written yesterday.

Archive note: project status, ratings, opinions, links and predictions are preserved as historical text from the old site. They are not silently updated to 2026.

Blog fragment

Bringing the World to Life Through Art: The Blockchain Metaverse

The surviving blog page opens by describing blockchain as a borderless, decentralized and trustless computing platform, then pairs that with virtual reality and a collective virtual shared space made of many individual worlds.

Its central idea is sovereign ownership: the blockchain metaverse extends the idea of shared virtual worlds by allowing ownership of virtual spaces rather than leaving the world entirely under one platform operator.
