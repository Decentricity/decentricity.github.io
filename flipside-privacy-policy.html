Flipside Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-03-01

This Privacy Policy explains how Flipside is operated and what data is (and is not) collected.

1. Data We Collect

We do not collect personal data from you through Flipside.

  • No account registration
  • No email collection
  • No analytics SDK tracking configured by us
  • No advertising identifiers used by us for profiling

2. Multiplayer and Network Features

Flipside may use anonymous GunDB peer-to-peer networking for multiplayer/game-state synchronization. This is designed to operate without requiring personally identifying account information.

3. Local Storage on Your Device

Flipside may store game-related data locally on your device (for example, settings or game progress). This data remains on your device unless you uninstall the app or clear app data.

4. Third-Party Services and Stores

When you download or purchase Flipside through app stores (such as Google Play), those platforms may process data under their own privacy policies. Please refer to those providers for details.

5. Children’s Privacy

Flipside does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted at this URL with a new effective date.

7. Contact

For privacy-related questions, please contact us through agent1c.ai.
